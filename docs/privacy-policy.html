Privacy Policy

Kick Counter (Baby Kick Tracker) · Last updated: 28 October 2026

Kick Counter ("the app") helps expectant parents count and review their baby's kicks. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app has no account, sign-up or login, and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your kick sessions (date, first and last kick time, duration and kick count) are saved only in the app's local storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Clear History, or by uninstalling the app.

Network use and third-party services

The app uses the internet only to check whether a newer version of the app is required. This check reads a small configuration value (the latest version number and update link) from Google Firebase Realtime Database. No personal data or kick data is sent as part of this check. Firebase may process standard technical information such as your IP address when the request is made, as described in Google's Firebase privacy information.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Permissions

The app requests only the Internet permission, used for the update check described above.

Sharing

We do not sell, trade or share your data with anyone. If you use Share App or Contact Us, your device's standard share sheet or email app opens, and what you send is under your control.

Medical disclaimer

Kick Counter is a tracking aid and is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice. Contact your doctor or midwife if you notice any change in your baby's movement pattern.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for expectant parents and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date above.
